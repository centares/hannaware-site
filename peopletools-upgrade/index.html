PeopleTools upgrade FAQ

What is the difference between a PeopleTools upgrade and a PeopleSoft application upgrade?

A PeopleTools upgrade moves the technical foundation (the PeopleTools release, middleware and supporting software) to a new version. Your application release, such as HCM 9.2 or FSCM 9.2, stays the same. Application updates are delivered separately through PeopleSoft Update Manager (PUM) images.

How long does a PeopleTools upgrade take?

It depends on how many applications you run, how far you are moving and how many customizations you have. We give you a timeline after the upgrade assessment, with the number of test passes agreed up front.

Can a PeopleTools upgrade be done remotely?

Yes. Hannaware delivers PeopleTools upgrades fully remotely, working with your infrastructure, database and functional teams.

Should we upgrade to PeopleTools 8.62 or go straight to 8.63?

That depends on your current release, your database and operating system certifications, and your timeline. Our PeopleTools 8.63 upgrade guide explains what to check before you decide.

Do you apply PUM images as part of the upgrade?

Yes. We can apply PeopleSoft Update Manager images and selected fixes alongside the PeopleTools upgrade, or schedule them as a separate update.

Can you upgrade PeopleSoft on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI)?

Yes. We provision and manage PeopleSoft environments on OCI with PeopleSoft Cloud Manager and the OCI CLI, and we also support AWS and on-premises servers.
